Out of scope entirely unless explicitly re-authorized: nutrition/calorie
tracking, social feeds, public leaderboards, XP/levels, paid APIs, telemetry,
AI coach, native iOS/Android builds in this phase.

"Move/reschedule" a planned workout was explicitly deferred in v0.0.5 (see
docs/DATA_MODEL.md) -- the current Program model has no calendar-occurrence
concept, and building one wasn't part of that version's scope.
