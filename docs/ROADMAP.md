Out of scope entirely unless explicitly re-authorized: nutrition/calorie
tracking, social feeds, public leaderboards, XP/levels, paid APIs, telemetry,
AI coach, native iOS/Android builds in this phase.

v0.0.6 is next (Progress & PRs) — not started; do not begin without a new
supervised session per the v0.0.5 handoff's hard stop. "Move/reschedule" a
planned workout was explicitly deferred in v0.0.5 (see docs/DATA_MODEL.md) --
the current Program model has no calendar-occurrence concept, and building
one wasn't part of this version's scope.
